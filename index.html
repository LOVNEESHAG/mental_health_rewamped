General notes

    Values you sent

    Estimates come from patterns in survey data and cannot diagnose anything. If you are struggling, speak to someone you trust or a qualified professional. In India, Tele-MANAS offers free support on 14416.

    About the model

    The model is a scikit-learn regression pipeline. It was trained on a public survey of 5,000 students covering age, gender, country, academic level, platform, purpose of use, daily screen time, phone unlocks, study, sleep, physical activity and stress.

    Each score is an average learned from similar students. Individual results vary widely, and the data reflects one survey at one point in time. Use the number to start a conversation about habits, and take health decisions to a qualified professional.
